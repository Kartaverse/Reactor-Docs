## Description
A paintout and cleanup helper for shots where traditional clone painting breaks down due to changing light, gradients, or uneven surfaces.


<p>Feed it a matte to isolate the area to remove, and the macro will generate a soft inpaint-style fill by extending surrounding edge information into the alpha and blending it with controllable blur.</p>

<p>Optionally, you can restore fine texture detail using the original plate or a separate texture input.</p>
